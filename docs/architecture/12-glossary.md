# 12. Glossary

- **Collection:** downloading, parsing, validating, and storing fuel-price observations.
- **Collector:** the server-side operation that downloads, parses, validates, and persists fuel prices when invoked.
- **Initial collection:** the collection attempt performed during startup; it must succeed for startup to succeed.
- **Price observation:** a fuel price associated with a station and observation timestamp.
- **Server lifecycle:** coordinated startup, operation, and shutdown of web delivery and collection requests.
- **Station history:** stored price observations for one fuel station.
